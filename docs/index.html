The strategy

Universe S&P 500 + Nasdaq-100, bought only once a stock is in either index.
Measure Relative P/E = P/E (price ÷ last 4 quarters' EPS) ÷ median P/E of its sector. A cheap stock needs less growth, an expensive one more.
Enter Trailing 12-month EPS growth ≥ 30% × relative P/E and each forecast fiscal year (current, next, year after) ≥ 30% × relative P/E, and never below the entry floor (15% × market P/E ÷ 19).
Exit Any forecast fiscal year < 25% × relative P/E, or below the exit floor (10% × market P/E ÷ 19). The floor (added Oct 2026) keeps very cheap, slow-growing stocks out; it scales with the median P/E of the whole universe against its 2002–26 average (19), so it rises in expensive markets (≈20% in 2021) and falls in cheap ones (≈12% in 2008–09). Backtests 2008–24 and 2022–26 both improved with no extra drawdown. The last, already reported year is shown but not required (requiring it made results worse).
Timing Act 3–14 days after each stock's earnings report (consensus revisions cluster there) or at the first close of each month (monthly sweep, which also resets equal weights).
Portfolio Equal weight across all stocks that qualify; cash if none.
12m target Expected 12-month peak = last close × (1 + forecast next-12m EPS growth) × P/E-model change. Shown as Upside, to judge how much room is left when buying. Not used to sell.

Real ETFs with related ideas

None beat QQQ by more than ~2%/yr over its life. QGRW (+1.7%/yr vs QQQ since Dec 2022) is the best; FFTY (reported growth + consensus + price strength, ~1,960% turnover) lost badly.

Caveats

  • Backtests use actual EPS plus simulated errors as the "forecast". Real consensus errs most at turning points; with strong anchoring the 2022–26 result drops to ~23%/yr (QQQ 14.6%).
  • Stocks removed from the indexes since 2022 are missing from the data (mostly losers), which flatters the backtests somewhat.
  • No minimum growth: very cheap stocks (low relative P/E) can qualify on modest growth.
  • v1.2 holds fewer stocks (about 18 in 2022–26, about 9 in the long run), so single names matter more.
  • This is a paper model portfolio for tracking the rule live. It does not place orders.
